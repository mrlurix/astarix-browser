Everything you need to browse

🗂

Tabs & navigation

Multi-tab browsing with per-tab history, reload, keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+T Ctrl+W Ctrl+L), page zoom from 30% to 300%, and a Chrome-style right-click menu.

🔍

Smart search

One omnibox for URLs and searches. Three engines — Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo — with live suggestions and one-click engine cycling.

🎨

Made yours

Dark and light themes, seven accent colors, custom start page background, bookmark shortcuts, your own homepage, and a default zoom level.

🛡

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. History, bookmarks, and settings stay on your machine. Risky permissions denied, downloads always ask first.

⬇

Downloads

Save-As flow with filename sanitization, a built-in download manager with progress states, and a custom downloads folder of your choice.

🧩

Extensions

Load unpacked Chromium extensions. Manifests are screened first — dangerous permissions and <all_urls> access are blocked.

Take a look

A faithful mockup of the real interface — try the theme switch, it works like the app.
