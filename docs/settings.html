Settings & Themes

Every option lives in one panel and applies instantly.

Opening Settings

Click the ⋮ menu in the toolbar, then ⚙ Settings. The panel slides in from the right; everything saves automatically.

Appearance

OptionChoices
ThemeDark (default) or Light
Accent ColorPurple, Blue, Green, Red, Orange, Pink, Teal
The accent color re-skins tabs, buttons, the new-tab page and highlights across the whole interface.

Startup

OptionWhat it does
Open with: New TabLaunch with a blank new tab (default)
Open with: HomepageLaunch straight into your Homepage URL
Homepage URLAny http(s) address, e.g. https://www.google.com

New Tab page

  • Show shortcuts — one-click tiles from your bookmarks (or curated defaults when you have none)
  • Background color — any color for the start page backdrop

Search

OptionChoices
Search engineGoogle, Bing, DuckDuckGo
Search suggestionsOn/off for live address-bar suggestions
Tip: click the engine badge next to the address bar to cycle engines without opening Settings.

Browsing

OptionChoices
Default zoom75% to 150%, applied to every page on load
Downloads folderAny absolute folder path, used as the starting point for Save dialogs

Performance

OptionWhat it does
Performance modeAuto (default), On, or Off
Auto turns the mode on by itself when the machine reports 2 CPU cores or less, or 2 GB of RAM or less. While active, Astarix drops animations, backdrop blur, shadows and the logo glow, which removes most of the GPU work and keeps the UI smooth on old or integrated graphics.

Reset all

Reset All Settings at the bottom of the panel restores every option to its default value.
